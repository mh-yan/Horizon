## [PenGPT: A Generative Transformer for Handwriting and Sketches](https://greydanus.github.io/2026/11/12/pengpt/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Sam Greydanus released PenGPT, an open-weights generative transformer that produces handwriting and sketches from text prompts. The project includes a 6M-parameter model for print/cursive handwriting that mimics unseen writers from a few words, and a 54M-parameter model for text-conditioned sketching. PenGPT demonstrates that strong generative results in handwriting and sketching can come from high-quality, large-scale datasets rather than novel architectures, reinforcing the 'Bitter Lesson' in machine learning. Its open-weights release enables applications such as handwriting tutoring and assistive tools, and provides a practical case study for data-centric model development. The handwriting model uses pen strokes snapped to an 8-direction grid with pen-up/down states, BPE tokenization of move sequences, and conditioning on SigLIP 2 vectors for sketching. The author notes that about 80% of the effort went into building and quality-controlling datasets, and community reports indicate limitations with non-English characters and premature stopping on some inputs.

**Background**: Handwriting synthesis is the task of generating realistic pen strokes that form text, often aiming to mimic a particular writer's style; it has traditionally been approached with GANs, RNNs, and diffusion models. The 'Bitter Lesson' is a well-known observation in AI that general methods scaling with computation tend to outperform approaches relying on domain-specific handcrafted knowledge. Transformers are a neural architecture that has become dominant in generative modeling across text, images, and other modalities.
